READ THE FULL STORY

JHIOS Soundtracks is an established business-facing branch of the wider JHIOS multimedia project, creating custom themes, background soundscapes and atmospheric audio for venues, shops and independent spaces.

Real talk on where things stand. This year the doors started closing. My old distributor stopped accepting new releases from me, and because I build with AI tools alongside my own writing and production, a lot of the big platforms (Apple Music, Shazam, YouTube Content ID, and the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok music libraries) won't carry new AI-assisted music at all. Everything already out stays live, and we're working to get new releases onto Spotify and a handful of other stores, but the old route of dropping a track everywhere at once is gone.

So JHIOS adapts. That's exactly why Infinite Exchange exists: an independent home for the work that doesn't need a gatekeeper's permission. JHIOS Soundtracks delivers direct to the venues, shops and businesses we make it for, and the archive keeps growing on our own terms.

Operating under Joseph Hrabec's independent Infinite Exchange ecosystem, built to bypass corporate streaming gatekeepers, JHIOS Soundtracks runs alongside its sibling JHIOS identities:

🎧 JHIOS Soundtracks: custom ambient themes, commercial background audio and concept scores. Releases include DAM After Dark and Shake Waffle & Rolls.

💀 SCR3WFAC3: raw energy, aggressive underground electronic and dark future soundscapes. Featured heavily on the core JHIOS playlist.

🌙 Luce Aether: transcendent, cinematic, ancestral and deeply atmospheric compositions. Collaborates across the network on projects from "Time I Wasted" to "Sanctum: The Astral Navigator".

♾️ Infinite Exchange: the parent independent archive and creator ecosystem being built as the official hub tracking the collective's journey.

JHIOS // INFINITE EXCHANGE

Infinite Exchange exists because too many artists are being limited, filtered, and controlled by corporate platforms. This is not just another streaming site. It is an independent archive and ecosystem where creators can release music, build directly with their audience, and own their infrastructure.

We document the journey — the struggle, the creation, and the evolution — because the story behind the sound matters.

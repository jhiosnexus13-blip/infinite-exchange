WHY WE'RE BUILDING THIS

Infinite Exchange exists because too many artists are being limited, filtered, and controlled by corporate platforms.

This is not just another streaming site. It is an independent archive and ecosystem where creators can release music, build directly with their audience, and own their infrastructure.

We document the journey — the struggle, the creation, and the evolution — because the story behind the sound matters.

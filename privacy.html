Privacy

We count plays by country. That's it.No IP addresses stored.No accounts, no ad trackers.

When a track has played for 30 seconds, this site records one play for the country your connection appears to come from (as reported by our host, Netlify). Only the two-letter country code and a running total are stored. These totals power the listener globe in the 3D world.

READ MORE

To stop one person inflating the numbers, a short-lived cookie (jx_l, 30 minutes, no personal data) limits how often plays from the same browser are counted.

Tracks currently play through an embedded YouTube player (privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com mode). YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that player.
